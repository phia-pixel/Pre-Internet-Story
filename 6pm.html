Now Canada gets cold, but it's rare for Southern Ontario to experience heavy snow prior to Christmas. As she pulled out from her apartment she felt her tires sliding beneath her and she could barely keep control of the car. She drove twenty feet down the road before giving up and with great difficulty, turned the car around. She even had trouble driving up the very small incline leading to the panel accessing her underground parking garage. My mom thought she’d have to climb out and push her car up the incline that was no bigger than a curb. After numerous attempts she finally made it to her parking spot and took herself back up to her apartment.
