My mother's stuffed animals in her apartment.

When my mom was in her mid-twenties, she made plans to meet up with a friend. They were born one day apart in December and since my mom’s boyfriend (my father) was in a different city studying for exams, she and this friend had decided to celebrate their birthdays together. A reservation was made at a restaurant about forty-five minutes by car from where my mother lived at the time. My mom wasn’t particularly thrilled about this arrangement, not because she didn’t want to see her friend, but because she wouldn’t get to see my dad. Perhaps it was due to this lack of enthusiasm that she hadn’t watched the news or listened to the radio to learn what kind of weather Toronto was expecting that mid-December day.
