DESIGNECOLOGIST, 2021

She knew that her friend, the determined person that she was, would be on her way to dinner, no matter the weather so my mom called the restaurant and asked them to let her know that my mother would be unable to make it. Feeling slightly crestfallen, my mother decided to go for a walk around the corner. Walking conditions were infinitely better than the driving ones. Strolling down the street, she took in the store windows with Christmas decor and lights illuminating the dark city around her.
